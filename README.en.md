## Introduction

This list collects tools related to novel / fiction writing:

- **AI Writing**: AI continuation, generation, polishing, outlining, plus general LLM apps you can use for free to write novels
- **Writing Software**: dedicated tools for writers such as Scrivener, yWriter, etc.
- **Creative Workflow**: outlining, worldbuilding, editing, formatting & publishing
- **Open Source Ecosystem**: self-hostable AI writing front-ends, local inference engines, open-source writing software and writing models
- **Communities & Resources**: writing communities, awesome lists, prompt libraries and tutorials

> ⚠️ Note: Pricing and status change frequently — always check the official sites. Tools marked "discontinued" should no longer be recommended.

## AI Novel Writing Tools (Chinese)

### AI Continuation / Generation

- [FeelFish](https://www.feelfish.com) - An AI novel writing agent (desktop) for novel creators: multi-agent collaboration, intelligent context continuation, one-click character & worldbuilding, supporting DeepSeek, ChatGPT, Kimi and other mainstream LLMs. (AI generation / Win·macOS·HarmonyOS PC / Credit-based)
- [Caiyun Xiaomeng (彩云小梦)](https://caiyunai.com) - A veteran Chinese AI writing tool launched in 2019; input the preceding text and it continues the story. One of the pioneers of Chinese AI continuation. (AI continuation / Web+App / Free tier + membership)
- [BiLing AI Writing (笔灵AI写作)](https://ibiling.cn) - A comprehensive Chinese AI writing platform with strong fiction features: outlines, character profiles, chapter continuation and style rewriting. (AI generation / Web / Subscription)
- [MaliangWriter (马良写作)](https://maliangwriter.com) - A multi-agent AI novel platform for Chinese web novels: three-level outlines, knowledge-graph consistency, long-form serialization; free outline/character generators. (AI generation / Web / Free + paid)
- [Spark Web Novel Assistant (星火网文助手)](https://www.sparkx.zone/tools/writerdesk.html) - iFlytek's web-novel-focused writing assistant (2024), designed for web novel authors: outlines, characters, chapter continuation, naming, polishing. (AI generation / Web / Mostly free)

### AI Writing Assistants / Polishing

- [Xiezuocat (秘塔写作猫)](https://xiezuocat.com) - Mita's Chinese AI writing assistant with strong proofreading, error correction and polishing; supports multiple genres including fiction. (AI polishing / Web+App / Free + membership)
- [iFlytek Zhiwen (讯飞智文)](https://zhiwen.xfyun.cn) - iFlytek's writing tool line; can generate stories and copy for free; reviews praise its story creation. (AI generation / Web / Mostly free)
- [Effidit (Tencent)](https://effidit.qq.com) - Tencent AI Lab's intelligent writing assistant: correction, polishing, completion and inspiration. Slowed updates after 2.0 (semi-dormant). (AI polishing / Web+plugins / Was free)

### General LLM Apps (usable for novel writing)

> General AI products, not fiction-specific, but commonly used for the whole outline-to-draft workflow.

- [Doubao (ByteDance)](https://www.doubao.com) - A leading free Chinese AI assistant with strong long-context ability; many community tutorials on "outline to finished draft" writing. (General AI / Web+App / Mostly free)
- [Kimi (Moonshot AI)](https://www.kimi.com) - Known for extremely long context; suitable for long novel chapters and setting documents; strong scores on creative benchmarks. (General AI / Web+App / Free + membership)
- [Tencent Yuanbao (腾讯元宝)](https://yuanbao.tencent.com) - Tencent's consumer AI assistant; added a "Writing Mode" in 2025 claiming to write novels of ten thousand words while chatting. (General AI / Web+App / Free + membership)
- [ERNIE Bot / Wenxin Yiyan (百度文心一言)](https://yiyan.baidu.com) - Baidu's LLM app; can generate character profiles, outlines and chapters (was renamed "Wenxiaoyan"). (General AI / Web+App / Free + membership)
- [Tongyi Qianwen (阿里通义千问)](https://tongyi.aliyun.com) - Alibaba's LLM app; can write stories and novels; fast-growing user base in 2025. (General AI / Web+App / Free + membership)
- [DeepSeek](https://www.deepseek.com) - The breakout open-source Chinese LLM of early 2025; free, strong reasoning and long context; widely used for inspiration, continuation and polishing; adopted by China Literature (阅文) and others. (General AI / Web+App / Mostly free)

### Official Platform Tools

> Platform tools: provided by the platform, usually for that platform's authors only.

- [Qidian Writer Assistant (起点作家助手, China Literature)](https://www.qidian.com) - Author app of China Literature; released the web-novel LLM "Yuewen Miaobi" in 2023, deployed DeepSeek in 2025 and launched the 10M-character "Miaobi Tongjian". (Platform tool / App / Free for platform authors)
- [Fanqie Writer Assistant (番茄作家助手, ByteDance)](https://fanqienovel.com) - Official author tool for Fanqie Novel with AI-assisted writing; the platform has explicit rules and chapter risk warnings for AI content. (Platform tool / App / Free for platform authors)
- [Qimao Writer Assistant (七猫作家助手)](https://www.qimao.com) - Official author tool of Qimao Free Novel for uploading and managing works, with basic writing aids. (Platform tool / App / Free for platform authors)

## AI Novel Writing Tools (International)

> International (mainly English) AI novel-writing tools. Pricing is public info; please check official sites.

- [Sudowrite](https://www.sudowrite.com) - The best-known international AI co-writing platform for fiction: Story Engine (outline-to-draft), Describe, Brainstorm, Rewrite, Feedback. (AI co-writing / Web / Subscription ~$10–29/mo)
- [NovelAI](https://novelai.net) - AI creation service focused on long-form novel text generation (Kayra model) plus anime-style illustration generation. (AI generation / Web / Subscription ~$10–25/mo)
- [Novelcrafter](https://novelcrafter.com) - AI-assisted writing platform that integrates multiple LLMs (OpenAI, Claude, Google, and local KoboldCpp/Ollama); scene-based manuscript management; bring your own API keys. (AI assistance / Web / Subscription)
- [DeepStory](https://www.deepstory.ai) - AI-assisted fiction writing tool known for its "Story Bible" to keep characters and worldbuilding consistent. (AI assistance / Web / Free trial + subscription)
- [NovelistAI](https://novelistai.com) - AI novel writing service emphasizing character consistency across chapters. (AI generation / Web / Free tier + subscription)

> 💡 General LLMs (e.g. Claude, ChatGPT) with good prompts can also cover the whole English novel-writing workflow; see [Communities & Resources](#communities--resources) for prompt libraries.

## Novel Writing Software

> Full writing software designed for authors (non-AI generation).

- [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) - The benchmark long-form novel writing app: outlining, chapters, research management, multi-document organization. (Writing software / Win·Mac·iOS / One-time ~$69)
- [yWriter](https://www.spacejock.com/yWriter.html) - Free Windows novel-writing software organized by chapters/scenes with word count stats. (Writing software / Windows / Free)
- [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com) - Cross-platform cloud novel writing software with clean UI; outlines, characters and worldbuilding. (Writing software / Web+App / Subscription ~$10–20/mo)
- [Wavemaker](https://wavemaker.cards) - Free, open-source web-based novel planning and writing software (PWA), all platforms. (Writing software / Web / Free, open source)
- [Squibler](https://www.squibler.io) - Web-based novel writing software with AI assistance and collaboration. (Writing software / Web / Subscription)
- [Novlr](https://www.novlr.org) - A web writing platform designed for novelists with a distraction-free interface; free tier available. (Writing software / Web / Free tier + subscription)
- [Ulysses](https://ulysses.app) - Elegant writing app in the Apple ecosystem; long-document organization and export. (Writing software / Mac·iOS / Subscription ~$40–50/yr)
- [iA Writer](https://ia.net/writer) - Minimalist, focused writing app; Markdown-friendly with syntax highlighting. (Writing software / Multi-platform / Low-cost subscription)
- [Effie](https://www.effie.co) - "Writing + mind map" app by a Chinese team; clean UI, Chinese-friendly. (Writing software / Multi-platform / Free + Pro)
- [novelWriter](https://github.com/vkbo/novelWriter) - Open-source plain-text editor designed for novel writing (Markdown-style) with outline, chapter and character management. (Writing software / Win·Mac·Linux / Free, GPL-3.0)
- [Manuskript](https://github.com/olivierkes/manuskript) - Open-source writing tool for authors: outline, character cards, mind-map-like structure. (Writing software / Multi-platform / Free, GPL-3.0)
- [bibisco](https://github.com/andreafeccomandi/bibisco) - Open-source novel writing software focused on character, plot, world analysis and narrative structure. (Writing software / Multi-platform / Free, GPL-3.0)
- [PureWriter (纯纯写作)](https://writer.drakeet.com) - Highly-rated Chinese writing app on Android; focused, loss-proof, long-text support. (Writing software / Android / Free + Pro)
- [Typora](https://typora.io) - Clean Markdown editor, good for writing and manuscript organization. (Writing software / Multi-platform / One-time $14.99)

## Outlining & Plot Planning

- [Plottr](https://plottr.com) - Visual plotting tool: timelines, plot templates, scene cards; integrates with Scrivener. (Outlining / Web+Desktop / Subscription)
- [Campfire](https://www.campfirewriting.com) - Modular writing and worldbuilding suite, module-based subscription (old one-time "Campfire Pro" discontinued). (Outlining/Worldbuilding / Web+Desktop / Subscription)
- [Fictionary](https://fictionary.co) - Structural editing tool for finished drafts; analyzes narrative structure scene by scene (analysis engine English-only for now). (Structural editing / Web / Trial + subscription)
- [Novelist](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=it.returntrue.novelist) - Free Android app for novel outlining and writing. (Outlining / Android / Free)

## Worldbuilding & Notes

- [World Anvil](https://www.worldanvil.com) - The most popular worldbuilding platform: lore entries, maps, timelines, character cards (free tier restricted since 2023). (Worldbuilding / Web / Free tier + subscription)
- [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) - Bidirectional-link knowledge base with rich community "novel writing" template libraries and the Substrate methodology (free for personal use). (Notes / Multi-platform / Free personal)
- [Notion](https://www.notion.com) - All-in-one notes and database tool; official template marketplace has many novel writing/worldbuilding templates. (Notes / Multi-platform / Free personal)

## Editing & Proofreading

- [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com) - All-round English writing checker: grammar, style, repetition, pacing analysis. (Editing / Web+plugins / Free tier + subscription ~$30/yr)
- [AutoCrit](https://autocrit.com) - Fiction-specific editing tool: scene pacing, dialogue, strong verbs and other fiction-focused analysis. (Editing / Web / Trial + subscription)
- [Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com) - Simple English readability checker that highlights long sentences and passive voice (free on web). (Editing / Web+Desktop / Web free, desktop $19.99)
- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com) - General English grammar checker (not fiction-specific, but widely used). (Editing / Multi-platform / Free tier + subscription)

## Formatting & Publishing

- [Vellum](https://vellum.pub) - The most professional ebook formatting software on Mac; one-click export to multiple ebook formats. (Formatting / Mac / One-time from ~$249)
- [Atticus](https://www.atticus.io) - Cross-platform web-based formatting and publishing tool; imports Scrivener files directly. (Formatting / Web / One-time $147)
- [Reedsy Studio](https://reedsy.com/studio) - Free online writing & formatting tool from Reedsy; chapter management, EPUB/PDF export. (Writing+Formatting / Web / Free)

## Open Source & Self-hosted

> Star counts measured on the research date and may change over time.

### AI Writing / Novel Generation

- [SillyTavern](https://github.com/SillyTavern/SillyTavern) - The most popular open-source LLM front-end: roleplay, long-form writing, world settings and memory. (AGPL-3.0 / ~33k★)
- [KoboldCpp](https://github.com/LostRuins/koboldcpp) - Single-file, zero-install GGUF model runner with a built-in KoboldAI writing UI; the most common entry point for local AI writing. (AGPL-3.0 / ~12k★)
- [textgen (formerly text-generation-webui)](https://github.com/oobabooga/textgen) - Veteran local LLM web front-end with comprehensive features. (AGPL-3.0 / ~48k★)
- [KoboldAI-Client](https://github.com/KoboldAI/KoboldAI-Client) - KoboldAI's classic browser client, a narrative/writing-focused UI. (AGPL-3.0 / ~3.9k★)
- [AIDungeon (open-source)](https://github.com/latitudegames/AIDungeon) - Early open-source version of the AI text adventure game, often used as a reference for AI storytelling. (MIT / ~3.2k★)
- [oh-story-claudecode](https://github.com/zenstory-ai/oh-story-claudecode) - A Chinese web-novel writing skill pack: ranking research, story deconstruction, writing, de-AI-flavoring, cover art. (MIT / ~6.1k★)
- [AI_NovelGenerator](https://github.com/YILING0013/AI_NovelGenerator) - Automatically generates multi-chapter long novels with context and foreshadowing continuity. (AGPL-3.0 / ~6k★)
- [AI-Writer (BlinkDL)](https://github.com/BlinkDL/AI-Writer) - Chinese novel generation project (fantasy, romance web novels) by the RWKV author. (Apache-2.0 / ~3.9k★)
- [chinese-novelist-skill](https://github.com/PenglongHuang/chinese-novelist-skill) - AI-driven Chinese fiction skill: three-level Q&A, cross-session preference memory, resume-after-interruption, chapter cliffhangers. (~2.7k★)
- [human-writing](https://github.com/KKKKhazix/human-writing) - A rewriting skill that makes AI-written Chinese read like a real person. (MIT / ~3.1k★)
- [AI-Novel-Writing-Assistant](https://github.com/ExplosiveCoderflome/AI-Novel-Writing-Assistant) - AI-native long-novel creation system: agents, worldview, writing-style engine, RAG, full-book production workflow. (Custom / ~2.6k★)
- [AI-automatically-generates-novels](https://github.com/wfcz10086/AI-automatically-generates-novels) - Intelligent novel creation productivity tool based on AI + prompts. (Apache-2.0 / ~900★)
- [MaliangAINovalWriter](https://github.com/Deng-m1/MaliangAINovalWriter) - AI novel writing tool for Chinese web novel authors (open-source counterpart of maliangwriter.com). (~860★)
- [QMAI (青幕AI写作)](https://github.com/Mochocyang/QMAI) - Focuses on character personality consistency in long novels to prevent OOC. (Custom / ~730★)
- [Openwrite](https://github.com/LiPu-jpg/Openwrite) - AI novel creation engine: conversation-guided project setup, multi-dimensional style extraction, quality review. (Apache-2.0 / ~640★)
- [Wordcraft (Google PAIR)](https://github.com/PAIR-code/wordcraft) - Google PAIR's AI short-story writing editor (prototype). (Apache-2.0 / ~310★)

### Local Inference Infrastructure (for running writing models)

- [Ollama](https://github.com/ollama/ollama) - One-command self-hosted runtime for open models (Qwen, RWKV, etc.); the easiest entry to local writing models. (MIT / ~179k★)
- [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp) - C/C++ LLM inference engine; the foundation of almost all local writing models (GGUF). (MIT / ~126k★)
- [Open WebUI](https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui) - User-friendly self-hosted LLM web UI supporting Ollama/OpenAI-compatible APIs; can serve as a writing chat station. (BSD-3 variant / ~150k★)
- [Jan](https://github.com/janhq/jan) - Local-first AI desktop client, usable offline. (Custom / ~44k★)
- [LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai) - Popular local model desktop client (closed-source commercial, for reference).

### Obsidian Writing Plugins

- [InkPilot](https://github.com/Nicholas-Yu/InkPilot) - Obsidian novelist plugin: AI planning, character design, outline generation, chapter writing and consistency review. (MIT)
- [eventide-quill](https://github.com/EventideMiles/eventide-quill) - Obsidian feedback-first writing assistant: local/cloud AI async review, style and continuity checks, plot branches. (MIT)

## Local Inference & Writing Models

> Open models usable for writing; licenses per repo / model card.

- [RWKV-LM](https://github.com/BlinkDL/RWKV-LM) - RNN-architecture LLM with long context and low VRAM; widely used by the community for novel continuation/generation. (Apache-2.0 / ~15k★)
- [Qwen3](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3) - Alibaba's Qwen series; strong Chinese writing ability. (Weights Apache-2.0 / ~28k★)
- [Yi](https://github.com/01-ai/Yi) - 01.AI's model series (Yi-34B etc.), usable for Chinese writing. (Apache-2.0 / ~7.8k★)

**Fiction-specific models on HuggingFace** (license per model card):

- [rwkv-novel-agent-g1h-13b-ctx10240](https://huggingface.co/xiaol/rwkv-novel-agent-g1h-13b-ctx10240) - RWKV novel-agent model (13B, 10K context).
- [rwkv-7B-world-novel-128k](https://huggingface.co/xiaol/rwkv-7B-world-novel-128k) - RWKV world-novel 7B (128K long context).
- [NaNovel-27B](https://huggingface.co/Dxniz/NaNovel-27B) - NaNovel 27B novel model.
- [qwen3-6-27b-chinese-realistic-fiction-lora](https://huggingface.co/yuxinlu1/qwen3-6-27b-chinese-realistic-fiction-lora-v1) - Chinese realistic-fiction LoRA (Qwen3.6-27B).

## Discontinued / Inactive

> Notable tools that have shut down or are clearly no longer maintained — listed so people don't waste time on them.

- **iFlytek AI Writing Assistant (科大讯飞AI写作助手)** - Officially shut down on **November 30, 2025** (do not confuse with the still-active "iFlytek Zhiwen").
- **Effidit (Tencent)** - Semi-dormant: no shutdown notice, but updates slowed noticeably after 2.0; Tencent's writing resources have shifted to Yuanbao.
- **Campfire Pro (old one-time version)** - Discontinued; Campfire is now module-based subscription.
- **Wavemaker v2 (Chrome version)** - Removed from the Chrome Web Store, replaced by wavemaker.cards (v4).
- **Caiyun Xiaomeng (彩云小梦)** - Still accessible, but popularity has dropped sharply; functionality is largely covered by general LLMs like Doubao and DeepSeek.
